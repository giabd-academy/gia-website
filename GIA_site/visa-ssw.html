How SSW Works

From language to landing a job

A clear, step-by-step route into a skilled job in Japan.

1

Japanese Level

Reach JFT-Basic or JLPT N4 — the language requirement for SSW.

⏱ 4–6 months
2

Skill Test

Prepare for and pass the skill test for your chosen sector.

⏱ 1–3 months
3

Job Matching

Get matched with a Japanese employer in your sector.

⏱ Varies
4

COE & Visa

Complete documents, get COE and your SSW work visa.

⏱ 2–4 months
5

Fly & Work

Travel to Japan and start your skilled job with ongoing support.

⏱ Departure
