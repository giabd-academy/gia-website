FAQ

Common Questions

I have a study gap or weak results — can I still go?

In many cases yes. With proper documents, financial planning and the right school, gaps and academic exceptions can often be handled.

What is the age requirement?

Usually after SSC/HSC. Specific eligibility depends on your pathway — we confirm after a profile review.

Do I have to pay everything at once?

No. Course fees can be paid in installments, and Japan-side costs are paid step by step.

Is Japanese difficult?

It feels new at first, but with regular classes most students are comfortable at N5 within 3–6 months.

Is the visa 100% guaranteed?

No honest agency can guarantee a visa. We prepare your file correctly — the decision is the embassy's.

Can I work in Japan?

Yes, up to 28 hours/week legally on a student visa. Better Japanese makes finding work easier.
