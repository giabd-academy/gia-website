About Us

Professional Study-in-Japan Support Center

Galaxy International Academy is a Japan-focused language and education support academy based in Chattogram, Bangladesh.


We help students prepare for Japan through Japanese language training, admission planning, document guidance, visa preparation support and post-arrival guidance.


Our goal is to make the Japan journey simple, transparent and student-friendly with trusted guidance from the beginning.
