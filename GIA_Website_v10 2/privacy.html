Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Galaxy International Academy ("GIA", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through our website and office, why we collect it, and your choices.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details — name, phone / WhatsApp, email, district.
  • Application details — date of birth, education, Japanese level, preferred course / service, intake, passport & sponsor status, message.
  • Tool results — mock-test score, cost estimate or eligibility answers if you choose to send them.
  • Admission & visa documents (office only) — certificates, passport / NID copy, photos, financial documents, when you enrol.
  • Website usage — if analytics is enabled, anonymous statistics (pages visited, device type) via Google Analytics / Meta Pixel cookies.

2. Why we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry, application, class or seminar.
  • To process admission to Japanese schools, COE, visa and SSW applications — only with your consent.
  • To keep accounts, receipts and legal records.
  • To improve our courses and website.

3. Who we share it with

Only when needed for your application: partner schools / employers in Japan, the Immigration Services Agency of Japan, the Embassy of Japan, and official test bodies (JLPT, NAT-TEST, JFT-Basic). We never sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

4. Storage & security

Data is stored in a secure cloud database (Google Firebase) and our office management system with role-based staff access and regular backups. Paper documents are kept in a locked office.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become admissions are kept for up to 2 years. Student records are kept as long as required for accounts and legal purposes.

6. Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of your data.
  • Ask us to correct wrong information.
  • Ask us to delete your enquiry data or withdraw consent for marketing.
